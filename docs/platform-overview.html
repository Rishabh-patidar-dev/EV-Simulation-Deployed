How the modules connect, and what moves between them

Every chart below is a flow the platform actually runs. Each box is a module you can open; each arrow is a record one module hands to the next. Diamonds are the points where the system decides, refuses, or waits for someone.

01

Module map

The modules that pass work to each other, grouped by the domain they belong to. Solid arrows are handovers. Dashed arrows are what a step raises on its own — an approval, an invoice, a voucher.

Customer relationship Dealer management ERP & integrations

Reading it: solid arrows are a record handed on — a quotation becomes a booking, a booking releases a delivery. Dashed arrows are raised automatically by the step they leave, without anyone asking for them.

02

Retail: enquiry to registered vehicle

The full customer journey as the platform runs it. Three decision points govern it: whether a discount needs approval, whether the loan is sanctioned before delivery can be released, and whether every vehicle is insured before dispatch.

CRM DMS ERP Decision

The temporary registration runs a clock. It is valid one month from handover and is counted down on screen, which is what forces the Vahan file to be closed rather than left open.

Dispatch is a gate, not a button. It is refused outright while any vehicle on the booking is uninsured, and refused again if the e-way bill connector is down — because the paperwork that must travel with the vehicle cannot be raised.

03

Supply: order to dealer stock

How vehicles reach the dealer the retail flow sells from. Three checks sit in front of every order — compliance, credit, and whether stock actually exists.

Dispatch is the moment the money moves. One click raises the GST invoice, adds it to what the dealer owes, moves the reserved vehicles into transit, writes the stock ledger entry and queues the accounting voucher. Rejecting the credit approval cancels the order instead.

04

Service and warranty

After-sales starts from the chassis number and finds everything else by itself — the owner, the dealer, the sale, the warranty position. Closing a job card splits the bill in two.

The claim is judged on arrival, not in a queue. Where the dealer supplies no odometer or battery reading, the rules engine takes the telematics reading from the vehicle itself.

05

Outside systems

Nine connectors, each with its own on/off switch and traffic log. Opening one on the API Integrations screen now shows what that third-party system is, in plain terms. Switch one off and the flows above fail where they would really fail.

WhatsApp Business

Meta WhatsApp Cloud API

Quotations, handover codes, service reminders and ticket replies. Business-initiated messages need a Meta-approved template; a customer reply opens a 24-hour free-form window.

Transactional SMS

DLT-registered route

The fallback when WhatsApp cannot deliver. TRAI requires the sender, the six-character header and every template to be registered on DLT before anything sends.

Payment gateway

Deployment's choice

Booking tokens and dealer collections. The platform sends a link and never touches card details; the gateway's capture callback is what marks a booking paid.

Telematics

Unit fitted at handover

Odometer, location, battery charge and state of health. These readings are what the warranty rules engine uses when a dealer supplies none.

Tally Prime

Accounting

Every invoice, receipt, credit note and payout queues a voucher. It posts only when the connection is up and every ledger it touches is mapped; otherwise it waits with its reason.

Vahan

Parivahan — Vahan 4.0 dealer point

Registration filed by the dealer: Forms 20, 21 and 22 with insurance, invoice and address proof. The number it returns is written back onto the vehicle.

GST e-invoice

NIC Invoice Registration Portal

Registers business-to-business tax invoices and returns the reference number and QR code. Retail sales to a customer without a GSTIN need none.

E-way bill

NIC E-Way Bill portal

Required before a vehicle can be dispatched. Where the invoice already carries its e-invoice reference, the bill is generated from it rather than re-keyed.

Customer ticketingNew

Raiseaticket

A cloud-hosted helpdesk for support tickets. Email becomes a ticket and replies thread onto it; tickets carry a priority, an SLA and a department, and escalate when the clock runs out — the behaviour Customer Support & Ticketing mirrors.

All nine are simulated in this build. Nothing leaves the application, but each connector behaves as the real one would, including its failure modes — messages bounce, filings are refused, vouchers queue.
